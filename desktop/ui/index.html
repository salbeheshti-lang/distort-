ℹ️ How It Works
DISTORT runs a local xray-core instance that creates a SOCKS5 + HTTP proxy on your machine. Your browser traffic is routed through this proxy → out through the selected VPN server. No data is logged, sold, or shared. All configs come from public community subscriptions.
